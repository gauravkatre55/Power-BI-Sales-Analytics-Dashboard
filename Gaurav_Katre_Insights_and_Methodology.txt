4. DATA MODEL ARCHITECTURE

The model uses separate fact and dimension tables rather than one flattened table.

Fact tables:
- Fact_Sales: sales order-line records and order quantities.
- Fact_Returns: returned quantities by return date, product, and territory.

Dimension tables:
- Dim_Calendar: date attributes.
- Dim_Product: product details, product cost, and product price.
- Dim_ProductSubcategory: subcategory names and category keys.
- Dim_ProductCategory: category names.
- Dim_Customer: customer attributes.
- Dim_Territory: region, country, and continent attributes.

Core relationships:
- Dim_Calendar[Date] → Fact_Sales[OrderDate] (active date relationship).
- Dim_Product[ProductKey] → Fact_Sales[ProductKey].
- Dim_Product[ProductKey] → Fact_Returns[ProductKey].
- Dim_Customer[CustomerKey] → Fact_Sales[CustomerKey].
- Dim_Territory[SalesTerritoryKey] → Fact_Sales[TerritoryKey].
- Dim_ProductCategory[ProductCategoryKey] → Dim_ProductSubcategory[ProductCategoryKey].
- Dim_ProductSubcategory[ProductSubcategoryKey] → Dim_Product[ProductSubcategoryKey].

Relationships should use one-to-many cardinality where the dimension key is unique and single-direction filtering unless a specific, justified requirement calls for otherwise. Ensure product/category filters reach Fact_Returns correctly.

5. KEY DAX MEASURES

Key DAX Measures Highlighted for Evaluation

The three most important measures in this project are Revenue, Profit Margin %, and YoY Revenue Growth Jan-Jun %. These demonstrate business KPI calculation, profitability analysis, and comparable year-over-year performance analysis. Other supporting measures are included in the Power BI report.

Revenue =
SUMX(
    Fact_Sales,
    Fact_Sales[OrderQuantity] * RELATED(Dim_Product[ProductPrice])
)
=============================================================================================
Total Cost =
SUMX(
    Fact_Sales,
    Fact_Sales[OrderQuantity] * RELATED(Dim_Product[ProductCost])
)
==============================================================================================
Total Profit = [Revenue] - [Total Cost]
==============================================================================================
Profit Margin % = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Revenue], 0)
==============================================================================================
Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(Fact_Sales[OrderNumber])
==============================================================================================
Total Quantity Sold = SUM(Fact_Sales[OrderQuantity])
==============================================================================================
Total Returns = SUM(Fact_Returns[ReturnQuantity])
==============================================================================================
Return Rate % = DIVIDE([Total Returns], [Total Quantity Sold], 0)
==============================================================================================
Comparable-period measures:

Revenue Jan-Jun =
VAR SelectedYear = SELECTEDVALUE(Dim_Calendar[Year])
RETURN
    CALCULATE(
        [Revenue],
        FILTER(
            ALL(Dim_Calendar),
            Dim_Calendar[Year] = SelectedYear
                && MONTH(Dim_Calendar[Date]) <= 6
        )
    )
==============================================================================================
Revenue Jan-Jun Previous Year =
VAR SelectedYear = SELECTEDVALUE(Dim_Calendar[Year])
RETURN
    CALCULATE(
        [Revenue],
        FILTER(
            ALL(Dim_Calendar),
            Dim_Calendar[Year] = SelectedYear - 1
                && MONTH(Dim_Calendar[Date]) <= 6
        )
    )
==============================================================================================
YoY Revenue Growth Jan-Jun % =
VAR CurrentRevenue = [Revenue Jan-Jun]
VAR PreviousRevenue = [Revenue Jan-Jun Previous Year]
RETURN
    IF(
        ISBLANK(PreviousRevenue),
        BLANK(),
        DIVIDE(CurrentRevenue - PreviousRevenue, PreviousRevenue)
    )
==============================================================================================

Measure note: Revenue and cost are calculated from order quantity multiplied by product price and product cost in the product lookup. These figures depend on the correctness of product keys, prices, and costs.

7. KEY BUSINESS FINDINGS

1. Revenue increased by 44.94% in 2021 compared with 2020, based on the dashboard's annual revenue comparison.
2. Total revenue across the available period is approximately 24.98M.
3. Overall profit margin is 42.08%.
4. Accessories has the highest displayed category profit margin at 62.84%, followed by Clothing at 44.27% and Bikes at 41.25%.
5. Bikes has the highest displayed category return rate at 3.07%. Clothing is at 2.16%, and Accessories is at 1.95%.
6. Accessories accounts for 61.85% of returned quantity in the category-share visual. This is a share of all returned quantity, not the same metric as category return rate.
7. Australia is the highest-revenue region in the displayed Top 5 Regions visual at approximately 7.4M. Southwest follows at approximately 4.8M.
8. Mountain-200 product variants appear among the highest-profit products.

Comparable January–June revenue shown in the dashboard:
- 2020: 3,748,976.82
- 2021: 2,974,825.81; change versus January–June 2020: -20.65%
- 2022: 9,185,655.39; change versus January–June 2021: +208.78%

January–June 2022 revenue is approximately 208.78% higher than January–June 2021 revenue based on the current dashboard figures. This unusually large increase requires validation against the source data, product prices, date filters, and DAX calculations before it is treated as a confirmed business trend. Both periods cover January through June, so the comparison uses the same six-month window.==========================================================================================================================================================================================
8. STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Improve revenue growth monitoring: Compare like-for-like periods. For 2022, use January–June against January–June in prior years rather than comparing six months with a full year. Investigate the unusually large 2022 increase before drawing conclusions.
2. Reduce avoidable product returns: Investigate the reasons for the higher Bikes return rate. Review product quality, fit, descriptions, packaging, and customer feedback before deciding on corrective action.
3. Grow profitable Accessories sales: Evaluate opportunities to increase Accessories sales while monitoring margin and return performance.
4. Improve regional performance: Review region-level revenue and profitability to identify growth opportunities and regions requiring further investigation.
5. Protect profitability: Use product-level profit and margin analysis to identify high-performing products. Review low-margin products, pricing, discounts, and costs before making changes.
6. Monitor performance consistently: Track revenue, profit, margin, orders, quantity, and return rates over time. Use consistent date windows and investigate anomalies before communicating conclusions.
